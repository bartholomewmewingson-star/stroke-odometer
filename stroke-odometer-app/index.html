    Install on your phone and keep your data safe

    There are no accounts. Everything is stored on this device and nothing is sent anywhere.

    • iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Do this, because Safari can clear data from sites you have not opened in about a week.
    • Android: open in Chrome, then use the menu and tap Install app.
    • The installed app and the browser tab keep separate data on some phones. Use Export backup and Import backup to move it.
    • Export a backup now and then. Clearing browser data or changing phones erases the log otherwise.
